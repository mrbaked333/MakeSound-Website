Four instruments

Synth, drums, sampler, and one that plays itself, all locked to the beat.

Photon

A two-oscillator synth with FM and a resonant filter. Play single keys or chords in any key, strummed or arpeggiated.

Impulse

A drum machine with six lanes of synthesized and sampled drums, a step sequencer, pads and swing.

Sampler

Record or import anything, then play it across the keys or on 16 pads. Auto-slice finds the hits for you.

Gravity

Orbs bounce around a room of walls, and every wall is a keyboard. It plays melodies, chords and rhythms on its own.

See it

Warm, tactile and made to be played.

Mix, arrange, finish

Everything you need to take an idea all the way.

A real mixer

Up to 32 tracks with faders, pan, mute and solo, mix buses, and a Maximizer to finish loud and clean.

Effects

Delay, Reverb, Drive, Filter, EQ, Chorus, Phaser, Bitcrusher, and a Compressor with sidechain.

Arrangement

Clips, loops and markers, a piano roll, and automation you can draw or record from the knobs.

Play it live

The XY Pad puts four effects under one finger. Drawable LFOs, count-in recording, and USB or Bluetooth MIDI keyboards.

Field gear

Foley, a tape-style field recorder, sends sounds straight to your song. The Analyzer reads tempo, note and key.

Desktop ready

Export the mixdown, 24-bit WAV stems and MIDI, or back up whole projects as a single file.
